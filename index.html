What is this app?

This is a small test of the Pretend Internet mini-app rules: every app should explain itself, work independently, and support different skins without changing its behavior. It does not connect to public networks or include unfinished Library projects. An installed Android APK has not yet been verified.

No account, server, or device permissions are required for this launcher.
