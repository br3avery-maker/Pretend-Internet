Pocket Notes — Help

What this is: A standalone offline notebook and scratchpad with Markdown preview, tags, pinning, and portable backups.

How to start: Click New note, enter a title and body, and optionally add comma-separated tags. Switch between Edit and Preview to inspect formatted headings, lists, checklists, and quotes.

What works: Instant local saving, search, tag filters, pinning, archiving, Undo delete, Dark and Daylight skins, and JSON or Markdown export/import.

Limitations: Plain-text/Markdown only (no embedded image attachments). Status is testing (browser-verified, not an installed Android APK release).

Where your data goes: All notes remain in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server or account. Use Export JSON to keep your own backup.
