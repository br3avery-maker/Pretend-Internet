    Pocket Reader — Help

    What this is: A standalone, offline-first chapter reader for Markdown, plain text, and sanitized HTML documents.

    How to start: Click Import file to load local .md, .txt, or .html files, paste text with New text, or try the Load synthetic sample button.

    What works: Chapter splitting, swipe left/right and keyboard arrow navigation, scroll-hiding Focus Mode, adjustable font size, serif/sans toggle, bookmarks, Dark and Daylight skins, and JSON shelf backup.

    Limitations: Imported HTML is converted to safe plain text and headings (scripts and external images are not executed). Binary EPUB/PDF archives are not parsed yet. Status is testing (browser-verified, not an installed Android APK release).

    Where your data goes: All readings, bookmarks, and preferences stay strictly in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded or synced over a network.
